# 🎉 Welcome to [Money Rate](https://moneyrate.lol)! 💸

## What is [Money Rate](https://moneyrate.lol)? 🤔

[Money Rate](https://moneyrate.lol) is your go-to app for converting fiat and crypto currencies in a snap! Whether you're a seasoned trader or just someone who wants to know how many tacos you can buy with your Bitcoin, we've got you covered! 🌮💰

It runs **entirely in your browser** — no accounts, no backend. Rates come from public APIs and your preferences live in `localStorage`, so it's fast and private. ⚡

## Features 🚀

- **📊 Real-time Currency Conversion**: Convert one base amount into many currencies, crypto, and commodities at once! No more guessing if you're getting a good deal!
- **🧭 Guided Onboarding Tour**: First-time visitor? A quick tour shows off the non-obvious tricks (tap a row to switch base, inline-edit the amount, search to add currencies, and more). Hit the **?** button anytime to replay it! Fully keyboard-friendly and localized.
- **📈 Interactive Charts**: Visualize your currency trends like a pro — with historical rates and CSV export!
- **🎢 Drag & Drop Currency Management**: Rearrange your currencies with a simple drag! Because who doesn't love a little chaos? 🎢
- **🔀 Shareable Links**: Copy a link that remembers your base, amount, and currency list — share your setup in one click!
- **🌗 Light / Dark Theme**: Easy on the eyes, day or night.
- **🌍 30 Languages**: Money talks — in your language.
- **📱 Installable PWA**: Add it to your home screen and keep converting even offline (last-known rates cached).
- **🗄️ Local Storage**: Your preferences are saved, so you can jump right back in without missing a beat! 🗄️

## Tech Stack 🛠️

Next.js 14 (App Router) · React 18 · TypeScript · Jotai · SWR · Tailwind + DaisyUI · driver.js (tour)
