<objective>
Fix the settings Reset button so it clears every persisted localStorage key — not just 10 of 12. Today the handler hardcodes a list that has drifted from the atom definitions, leaving `tourSeen` and `showDatePicker` behind. Because `tourSeen` survives Reset, the auto-start gate at `src/app/page.tsx` (`if (!hydrated || tourSeen || ...) return`) still sees `tourSeen === true`, so the onboarding tour never replays after a Reset.

The fix removes the drift by introducing a single source of truth in `atoms.ts` and consuming it from the Reset handler, plus a drift-guard unit test so future atoms cannot silently reintroduce the bug.

Purpose: Restore expected Reset behavior (tour replays, date-picker toggle clears) and prevent recurrence.
Output: `PERSISTED_ATOM_KEYS` constant, updated Reset handler, and a coverage test.
</objective>
